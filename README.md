Bring the Pain is a free, no-signup workout tracker built for your browser — no app store, no subscription. Log sets with weight, reps, or run distance and time, and watch your streak, weekly totals, and lifetime numbers update automatically. Build a weekly training plan exercise by exercise, with target weights, rep ranges, rest periods, and rounds for circuit days, or paste in a workout and let it auto-fill your plan. A rest timer and session stopwatch keep you on pace mid-workout, while separate tabs track body weight, height, BMI, and daily steps with simple trend charts. Share a quick progress summary straight from your phone, or hand someone a QR code to open the app themselves. Everything saves privately in your own browser and never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.
