Quick reference

BMR — Mifflin-St Jeor equation
Men: 10×weight(kg) + 6.25×height(cm) − 5×age + 5
Women: 10×weight(kg) + 6.25×height(cm) − 5×age − 161
TDEE — total daily energy expenditure
BMR × activity multiplier (1.2 sedentary → 1.9 very active). This is roughly how many calories you burn in a day, including exercise.
Calories per gram
Protein: 4 kcal/g  ·  Carbs: 4 kcal/g  ·  Fat: 9 kcal/g
Rule of thumb
A pool of about 3,500 calories ≈ 1 lb of body fat. A 500 cal/day deficit or surplus is roughly 1 lb/week — a pace generally considered sustainable rather than extreme.
These are population-average estimates, not a medical calculation — actual needs vary by individual. This isn't medical or nutrition advice; check with a doctor or registered dietitian before making significant changes, especially with any health condition.

Community

Community is optional and needs a free account so posts can be matched to a name and synced between everyone in the group. Nothing here affects your personal tracking — Log, BMI, Nutrition, etc. all keep working fully offline either way.
How to get in
  1. Open the Account tab.
  2. The first time anyone uses this, Sign up with an email and a password (6+ characters) — this is your own personal login, not shared with coworkers.
  3. Already have an account from another device? Use Log in instead with the same email and password.
  4. Once logged in, come back to this Community tab — Share a win, the Feed, Leaderboard, and Group Challenge all unlock automatically.
Logging in only connects this device to your account for syncing and community features — your workout data stays private to you unless you choose to post it here.

Group Challenge

Create or join a private body-composition challenge with coworkers. Only your % body fat change and fat mass lost since you joined are ever shared with the group — your actual weight, body fat %, and BMI never leave this device.
Starting a new challenge
  1. Type a name (e.g. "Q4 Wellness Challenge") and tap Create a challenge.
  2. You'll get a 6-character join code — it appears at the top of the challenge once it's created.
  3. Send that code to coworkers however you'd normally share a code (Slack, text, email). Anyone with the code can join — there's no separate invite list to manage.
  4. You're automatically a member of the challenge you create, same as anyone who joins with the code.
Joining a coworker's challenge
  1. Get the join code from whoever created it.
  2. Type the code into the Join code field (not case-sensitive) and tap Join with code.
  3. You can only be in one challenge at a time — leave your current one first if you want to switch.

Group Challenge

Leaving removes you from the dashboard and stops sharing your changes — your logged body composition data itself is untouched and stays right here on your device.

Install as an app

This page is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — once it's hosted online, you can add it to your home screen and it opens full-screen, just like an app from the store.
📱 iPhone (Safari)
  1. Open this page's link in Safari (not Chrome, not Gmail/Messages in-app browser — Add to Home Screen only shows up in actual Safari on iOS).
  2. Find the Share icon — on iPhone it's in the toolbar along the bottom of the screen: a square with an arrow pointing straight up out of it, usually the middle icon of the three along the bottom bar.
  3. Don't see a toolbar at all? Tap once near the bottom of the screen, or scroll the page up slightly — Safari hides the toolbar while you scroll down and brings it back when you scroll up or tap.
  4. Tap the Share icon. A menu slides up from the bottom.
  5. In that menu, scroll down through the row of app icons and tap Add to Home Screen (it has a small "+" square icon). If you don't see it, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of that list and add it, or just keep scrolling — it's usually a few rows down.
  6. Tap Add in the top right corner. The app icon now appears on your home screen like any other app.
📱 iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this page's link in Safari on the iPad (not Chrome).
  2. On iPad the Share icon is in the toolbar at the top of the screen, near the address bar — same square-with-an-up-arrow icon, usually to the right of the address bar.
  3. Tap it, then scroll down in the menu that appears and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right. The icon appears on your home screen.
🤖 Android (Chrome)
  1. Open this page's link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right, or wait for the "Install app" banner at the bottom.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Confirm by tapping Install.
Once installed, the app works offline and remembers your data on that device — scan the QR code above or send yourself the link to get started.

Update workouts on laptop & phone

This app saves everything on the device you're using — there's no account or cloud sync. If you edit your Training Program on your laptop, your phone's installed app won't see the change (and vice versa) until you move a backup file across.
💻 Updated on your laptop? Push it to your phone
  1. On your laptop, tap Export backup below — it downloads a small .json file.
  2. Send that file to your phone however you normally would (email it to yourself, AirDrop, a cloud drive, Messages).
  3. Open the file on your phone, or open the app there and tap Import backup, then choose the file.
  4. Your phone now matches your laptop. This replaces what's on the phone, so export from your laptop last if you've made changes on both.
📱 Updated on your phone? Push it to your laptop
  1. On your phone, open the app and tap Export backup below.
  2. Send the downloaded file to your laptop the same way (email, cloud drive, AirDrop/Nearby Share).
  3. On your laptop, open the app, go to Share → Import backup, and choose the file.
